01 / GLOBAL CHALLENGE

Our oceans are connected to every community.

Marine ecosystems face multiple pressures. Pollution can travel through rivers, coastlines and ocean currents, while habitat degradation and unsustainable activities threaten biodiversity.

♻

Marine Plastic Pollution

Mismanaged plastic waste can move from settlements and waterways into estuaries and coastal ecosystems, where it can persist and harm marine life.

🧪

Water Contamination

Chemical discharges, wastewater and nutrient pollution can degrade aquatic environments and create risks for ecosystems.

🐠

Biodiversity Loss

Habitat degradation, overexploitation and pollution place pressure on coral reefs, fish populations and marine species.

🌡

Ocean Warming

Rising ocean temperatures and marine heatwaves can stress sensitive habitats and contribute to coral bleaching.

🏝

Coastal Ecosystem Degradation

Mangroves, salt marshes and seagrass habitats face pressures from development, pollution and changing environmental conditions.

📊

Data Gaps

Environmental observations are often scattered across different sources, making it difficult to compare locations and coordinate action.

12 / PROJECT SUMMARY

BlueSentinel — a connected view of marine challenges.

BlueSentinel is a proposed global marine intelligence and decision-support platform focused on the connection between river systems, coastal environments and ocean health. Its prototype combines geographic selection, transparent pollution-pressure screening, hypothetical plastic leakage scenarios, marine environmental context and conservation planning. By bringing these elements together, the project aims to help users understand pollution pathways, organise environmental information and explore practical interventions. Future development will require verified datasets, scientific validation, community partnerships and real-world pilot studies.
